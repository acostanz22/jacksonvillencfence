Wood fencing

Wood fence installation in Jacksonville, NC

Privacy, picket, and shadowbox wood for Jacksonville yards that want a solid screen and a fence you can stain to the house. We measure the line, set the posts for sand, and price the gates separately.

When this fence is the right job

Choose wood when the job is a backyard screen, a stained match to the house, or a repair on a wood line that still has sound posts. Inside the City of Jacksonville, side and rear residential fences are limited to 6 feet, and front-yard fences are limited to 4 feet. A tall privacy fence belongs behind the front wall of the house.

How the work goes

  1. 1Walk the line. We note the slope, trees, sprinklers, gates, and who owns the property line.
  2. 2Check the rule. City height limits, county easements, and any HOA color or material sheet come before the lumber order.
  3. 3Set the posts. Posts go in concrete, crowned at the top, deep enough for coastal-plain sand.
  4. 4Hang the fence and the gate. Rails, boards, and a gate that latches without dragging on the ground.
  5. 5Walk it with you. We point out what to stain and which boards sit closest to sprinklers.

A privacy style sets the boards tight so the yard is screened. Shadowbox staggers boards on both faces so neighbors on each side see a finished side and a little air can pass. Picket is the shorter open fence for a front yard. The style is part of the quote, because board count and both-side nailing change the material.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Length and height

A 6-foot privacy run uses more board than a 4-foot picket on the same line.

Style

Privacy, shadowbox, and picket do not share a board count or a rail layout.

Gates

Each walk gate and drive gate is its own frame, posts, and hardware.

Tear-out

Hauling off an old fence, including posts in concrete, is a separate piece of the job.

Ground

Slope, roots, and soft spots add time. A flat side yard does not price like a ditch line.

Access

A long carry from the street, or a backyard with no gate yet, takes more labor.

Questions about wood fencing

How long does a wood fence hold up in Jacksonville humidity?

Humidity and summer rain are what age a bare board. A finish renewed before the wood cracks is what keeps a privacy fence in service. Shade, sprinklers, and how often you stain it matter more than a catalog year-count, so we do not promise a lifespan.

Can you repair part of a wood fence instead of replacing the line?

Yes, when the posts are still solid. We can replace rails, boards, or a gate and leave the sound sections. If a post has rotted at the ground, new boards on that post will lean again. We say which it is after we see the line.

What is the difference between privacy, shadowbox, and picket?

Privacy boards sit tight and screen the yard. Shadowbox staggers boards on both sides of the rail so each neighbor sees a finished face. Picket is a shorter, open fence used where a front yard has a lower height limit.

Do wood posts need concrete in Onslow County sand?

Yes on a residential line. Coastal-plain sand does not grip a post the way clay does. We set posts in concrete and crown the top so water does not sit against the wood.

Will a solid wood fence handle wind off the coast?

A solid fence catches wind. On an open lot we space posts for that load instead of stretching a tall run between too few posts. Shadowbox lets some air through and is sometimes the better build on an exposed side line.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
