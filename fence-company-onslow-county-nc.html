What fence work looks like here

Inside Jacksonville, residential front-yard fences are limited to 4 feet and side or rear fences to 6 feet, and nonresidential fences may go to 8 feet. In unincorporated county, a zoning permit is not required for a fence, the ordinance does not set a standard fence location, and electric or barbed wire on a house lot stays on the interior and is marked if it is close to a property line. Pools and spas that hold 24 inches of water or more need a barrier at least 4 feet high. HOA rules can be tighter than both the city and the county. We build to the rule that applies to the parcel in front of us.

Do I need a fence permit in unincorporated Onslow County?

The county says a zoning permit is not required for a fence. Keep it off easements and the right-of-way. Talk to the neighbor if you plan to build on the line. Towns inside the county can have their own height rules.

What are the City of Jacksonville fence heights?

Published city standards limit residential fences to 4 feet in the front yard and 6 feet on the side and rear, and nonresidential fences to 8 feet. Nothing may block a sidewalk, sit in an easement, or break a corner sight line.

Which fence belongs on a county acreage lot?

Farm fence on the long lines, and wood, vinyl, or chain link around the house yard if you want a screen or a dog run. See the farm page for horses versus cattle.
