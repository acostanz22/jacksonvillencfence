What fence work looks like here

Wood still works when it will be stained and the posts are in concrete. Vinyl and aluminum ask less of you next to the water. Vinyl-coated chain link is the chain-link spec we lean toward this close to the sound. Corner lots need a fence that stays out of the sight line for drivers. We measure the opening for the gate before we order it, because a side yard in town rarely has extra room.

Salt and coatings

Aluminum, vinyl, and coated chain link are the materials we specify first on lots that take spray and humidity off the water.

Tight lines

Short side yards mean the gate width and the post layout have to be exact. There is not much room to adjust after the concrete is in.

Town rules

Confirm Swansboro’s height and setback for your street, especially on a corner. We do not copy a Jacksonville city limit onto a Swansboro lot.

Can you install a fence close to Bogue Sound?

Yes, on private lots. We look at soft ground and standing water before posts go in, and we spec coatings that handle salt better than bare steel.

Is a wood privacy fence a bad idea in Swansboro?

It is a fine screen if you will stain it and the posts are set in concrete. If you do not want that upkeep, vinyl privacy is the closer match.

Do Jacksonville’s 4-foot and 6-foot limits apply here?

Those numbers are the City of Jacksonville standard. Swansboro is its own town. Bring the local rule, or we will confirm it before we order a height.
