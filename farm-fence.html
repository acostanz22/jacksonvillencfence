Farm fencing

Farm fence installation near Jacksonville, NC

Rail, woven wire, pipe-and-cable, and electric fence for horses, cattle, and other animals on acreage around Jacksonville. The animal decides the fence. A backyard privacy panel does not.

When this fence is the right job

This is the page for a pasture, a garden you need to keep deer or livestock out of, or a long property line in Richlands, Hubert, or rural Onslow County. A horse needs a fence it can see. Cattle can be held with wire or electric. Split rail marks a line and looks right, and it holds animals only when the right wire is added.

How the work goes

  1. 1Name the animals. Horses, cattle, goats, and a garden perimeter are different fences.
  2. 2Walk the corners and gates. Brace corners and the openings for a truck or a tractor carry the line.
  3. 3Clear the path. Brush on the line comes out before wire goes up. We include that when it is part of the job.
  4. 4Set posts and hang the fence. Depth follows the soil. Soft, wet ground gets a different post plan than a firm field.
  5. 5Check gates and energizers. Electric fence is tested. Gates are hung so a truck can actually use them.

Pipe-and-cable is a horse fence owners ask for because the rail is visible and the cable gives. Woven wire and high-tensile electric are the practical cattle options. Barbed wire is a cattle tool, not a horse fence, and on a house lot Onslow County wants barbed or electric wire on the interior side of the property and marked when it sits close to a line. A bona fide farm is treated differently. We sort that out for the parcel.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Length

Acreage lines are priced on the run, and corners add bracing.

Animal

A horse fence and a cattle fence do not share posts, wire, or spacing.

Gates

A 12-foot farm gate is a different piece from a backyard walk gate.

Clearing

Fence in the woods costs more than fence in an open field.

Ground

Wet spots and sand change post depth and how many posts we set.

Power

An electric line needs an energizer, ground rods, and a place to put them.

Questions about farm fencing

What fence fits horses versus cattle?

Horses need a fence they can see and that will not cut them: pipe-and-cable, rail, or a no-climb mesh with a visible top. Cattle can be held with woven wire, barbed wire, or high-tensile electric. A horse should not live on a cattle barbed-wire line.

Is split rail enough to hold animals?

Split rail marks a line and suits a pasture edge. It does not hold a determined horse or a small goat unless wire is added. We say which animals the style will actually contain.

Do farm fences need a permit in Onslow County?

The county does not require a zoning permit for a fence, and bona fide farms are treated differently from a house lot on electric and barbed wire. Inside a town, ask before you build. Easements and the right-of-way are still off limits.

Can you mix a privacy fence by the house with wire on the back acreage?

Yes. That is a common layout in Hubert and Richlands: wood or vinyl around the yard, and a farm fence on the longer field lines. They are two materials and two prices.

What changes a farm-fence estimate the most?

Length, then corners, gates for trucks, brush that has to come off the line, and soft ground. A long wire run is not priced like a backyard.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
