Fence company in Holly Ridge, NC

Holly Ridge house lots are a mix of newer subdivisions and older parcels between Jacksonville and the beaches. The fence question is usually an HOA color, a 6-foot rear privacy line, and posts that will hold in sand.

What fence work looks like here

We ask for the written HOA rule before ordering white vinyl, stained wood, or a black aluminum front fence. Front yards and corner sight lines are where those rules bite. The ground is still sandy, and a new subdivision lot can hide irrigation lines along the fence path. Flag those before we dig. This is residential work first: wood, vinyl, and aluminum, with chain link where a dog run or a side yard does not need a solid screen.

HOA sheets

Height, color, and which side faces the neighbor should be in writing before material is ordered.

New-lot utilities

Irrigation and shallow utilities are common on fresh subdivisions. We need them marked.

Privacy on the rear line

Most of the screen goes on the side and rear. The front stays within whatever the town and the HOA allow.

Can you match an HOA fence color in Holly Ridge?

If the HOA names a color or a material, we order that. Send the sheet with the estimate request so the quote uses the right product.

Is chain link allowed in Holly Ridge subdivisions?

Some allow it on the rear line and some do not. The covenants decide. We will not guess from the fence next door.

Do posts in newer Holly Ridge yards need concrete?

Yes. The soil is sandy. Concrete and a crowned top are how a post stays put after rain.
