Privacy

What we do with the details you send when you ask for a fence estimate.

The quote form on this site is provided by Tally. When you submit it, we receive your name, phone number, email, and whatever you wrote about the job, along with a source field that tells us the lead came from Jacksonville NC Fence Company.

We use that information to reply about a fence estimate and to schedule a look at the property. We do not sell the form contents. Phone calls to (910) 519-2447 are answered for the same purpose.

If you want your request deleted, call or write and say so. Do not send payment information through the form.
