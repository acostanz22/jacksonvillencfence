Fence company in Richlands, NC

Richlands sits inland of Jacksonville, with larger lots and more pasture than the neighborhoods along the river. Fence work here is often a long line: field wire, rail, or a privacy screen around the house and a different fence on the back acreage.

What fence work looks like here

Salt is less of a factor than it is in Sneads Ferry, so galvanized chain link and wood are common and sensible. The soil is still coastal-plain sand in many fields, and posts still go in concrete. A mile of wire is quoted on corners, gates for trucks, and brush, not on a backyard per-foot habit. Inside the town, ask about local height rules. Outside town limits, Onslow County does not require a fence zoning permit.

Longer runs

Acreage changes the quote. Corners, braces, and farm gates matter more than picket style.

Two fences, one property

A wood or vinyl yard around the house and wire on the field is a normal Richlands layout.

Less salt, same sand

Coatings can be simpler than on the coast. Post depth still has to suit sand.

Do you build horse and cattle fence in Richlands?

Yes. Horses get a visible fence such as pipe-and-cable or rail. Cattle can use woven wire or electric. We do not put horses on barbed wire.

Can the house yard and the field be different fences?

Yes. They are quoted as two materials. The privacy line stops where the pasture fence starts.

Is a county permit required outside Richlands town limits?

Onslow County says a zoning permit is not required for a fence. Stay off easements and the right-of-way, and check with the town if the lot is inside town limits.
