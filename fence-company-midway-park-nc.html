Fence company in Midway Park, NC

Midway Park work is off-base housing along the corridor toward Camp Lejeune. The jobs are practical: pet containment, a privacy line on a modest yard, and repairs after a storm. We do not install inside the base gate.

What fence work looks like here

Many houses are rentals. We need the owner’s approval, or a tenant who has it in writing, before we set posts. A landlord who wants the fence removed later should say that up front so the spec matches. Yards are often smaller than Hubert acreage, so chain link, wood privacy, and vinyl are the materials that fit. Timelines around a move are easier when the line is simple and the gate count is low.

Off base only

We fence private houses in Midway Park. Base housing and the installation itself are outside this work.

Rental fences start with written permission from the owner.

Useful, not decorative

Can you install a fence on Camp Lejeune?

No. Our work in this area is on off-base private property in and around Midway Park.

I rent. Can I still get a fence?

Only with the owner’s written permission. Send that with the request so the estimate is for a job we are allowed to do.

What fence makes sense for a dog in a small Midway Park yard?

Chain link or a short wood or vinyl run around the part of the yard the dog uses. A full privacy line is there if you also want the screen. The gate latch matters as much as the height.
